While it is on, this applies to all sites. Any page you open can read responses from other sites that don't need a sign-in, including services on your local network.

What it does

Adds Access-Control-Allow-* headers to fetch and XHR responses, so the browser lets your page read them.

How to use it

Click the toolbar icon to switch it on or off. The icon turns blue and shows ON while it is active.
